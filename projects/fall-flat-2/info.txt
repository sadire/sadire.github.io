In this project I worked as a middle point between design and programming. As a technical designer my main focus was to prototype and iterate mechanics and gameplay moments. I also worked on level implementation and testing what worked and what didn't.

[intro es]
En este proyecto trabajé a medio camino entre diseño y programación. Como technical designer mi foco principal era prototipar e iterar mecánicas y gameplay moments. También trabajé en la implementación de niveles y en probar qué funcionaba y qué no.

[detail en]
I wrote feature requests for the code team and built sample zones for the different tools, exploring their limits and how they could fit into the game.

I was also part of the level design process, helping shape the levels early on alongside the designers.

The role also included QA responsibilities. In that department I took care of manual testing, finding and tracking bugs and keeping the builds stable.

[detail es]

También participé en el proceso de diseño de niveles, ayudando a dar forma a los niveles desde el principio junto a los diseñadores.

El rol incluía además responsabilidades de QA. En ese departamento me encargaba del testeo manual, encontrando y registrando bugs y manteniendo las builds estables.
